✏️ Words

Your words, your song

Each part of the song gets its own coloured box — verse, chorus, bridge. Stuck for a line? Tap Ideas for rhymes and next lines, and pick the one you like.

  • Say it instead of typing it — speech-to-text built in
  • Rhyme and next-line ideas, never written for you
  • Undo for everything, so nothing is ever lost
🎨 Style

Pick a sound — or describe it

Tap a picture card to hear a quick taste. Choose a mood face and an animal speed, or just tell Musix in your own words: “spooky but bouncy, like a haunted fairground.”

  • Twelve styles, from pop and rock to lullaby and reggae
  • Change instruments, chords, speed and key whenever you like
  • Optional “magic band” for a studio-sounding backing track

For grown-ups

Musix is free and open source. The song-making AI runs through OpenRouter, using your own key — so you stay in control of what it costs.

Getting started

  1. Download and install Musix (buttons above).
  2. Create a key at openrouter.ai/keys — you can set a spending limit on it.
  3. In Musix, open ⚙️ Settings, press and hold Grown-ups, answer the sum, and paste the key.

Without a key Musix still works: a built-in composer writes the music, words are typed instead of spoken, and the computer’s own voice reads aloud.

What it costs

Composing a song and speech-to-text cost fractions of a cent. Natural read-aloud is about $0.0002 per phrase, then free once it’s been heard. “Magic band” tracks and cover pictures cost about $0.08 each, with a daily limit you set.

First launch

Mac: right-click the app and choose Open the first time.
Windows: if SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.
